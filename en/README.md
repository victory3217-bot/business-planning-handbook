## Purpose of This Handbook

This is a draft public handbook that reorganizes the 8 core areas of business planning — setting your criteria, exploring business opportunities, defining the customer and the problem, building the value proposition / competitive advantage / positioning, designing the business model, designing cost / pricing / the revenue model, validating business feasibility and the Effective Market, and executing / validating / pivoting — into a single connected Learning Path. Rather than starting from a specific item or product, it follows the judgment sequence: Criteria → Opportunity → Customer → Value Proposition → Business Model → Pricing → Feasibility → Execution & Validation.

## Target Audience

Aspiring and early-stage founders learning a systematic approach to business development for the first time, or non-specialist staff responsible for early-stage business planning. No specific industry experience or technical expertise is assumed.
