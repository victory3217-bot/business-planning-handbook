## Purpose of This Handbook

This handbook reorganizes the 8 core areas of business planning — setting your criteria, exploring business opportunities, defining the customer and the problem, building the value proposition / competitive advantage / positioning, designing the business model, designing cost / pricing / the revenue model, validating business feasibility and the Effective Market, and executing / validating / pivoting — into a single connected Learning Path. Rather than starting from a specific item or product, it follows the judgment sequence: Criteria → Opportunity → Customer → Value Proposition → Business Model → Pricing → Feasibility → Execution & Validation.

## Target Audience

Corporate new-business and strategy-planning teams, in-house venture teams, and business-unit planners. Startups and corporate new-business teams alike can plan a business through the same eight-step judgment sequence, and non-specialist staff responsible for early-stage business planning can read it too. No specific industry experience or technical expertise is assumed.

The companion book, 《스타트업을 하는 깐깐한 방법》 (*The Meticulous Way to Do a Startup*), covers the same eight steps of business planning, which apply just as well to corporate new businesses.

## Appendices (Supplementary Material)

Outside the eight chapters, practical methods drawn from the standard lecture materials, which organize the author's teaching and consulting results, are collected as appendices. They are kept separate from the body (which is Knowledge-Module based).

- Appendix A. Business Plan Writing in Practice
- Appendix B. Customer Interviews, Observation, and Empathy Research
- Appendix C. A Framework for Channel Decisions
- Appendix D. B2B and B2G Sales: The Basic Framework
- Appendix E. The Investor Perspective: What Investors Read and Ask in a Plan (draft)
- Appendix F. The Operating Model: Who Runs It, in What Order, by What Rules (draft)

Appendices E and F are drafts that rest on a single set of standard lecture materials (a small number of decks). The check that would tie their basis to the original sources page by page is not yet complete, and Appendix E is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Appendix F is limited to tools and questions for checking the operating structure and has no operating cases.

## Source Policy

This handbook reorganizes content from the author's internal Knowledge Base into textbook form for learners, without external research, web search, or general-knowledge supplementation. Afterwards, the standard lecture materials that organize the author's teaching and consulting results were added as a second source, and some chapters were supplemented from them. Supplemented parts are marked "Supplementary source" in each chapter's Source Map. Information that could not be confirmed in the lecture materials (fees, regulations, and statistics that depend on a reference date), practice-only sample data, and personally or corporately identifying information are not included. See [`../source-map.md`](../source-map.md) for the chapter-to-source mapping.
