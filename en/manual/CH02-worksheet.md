**Product Risk** — Does the customer problem connect to the capability to solve it?
```

Basis: (write here)
```

**Channel Risk** — Even with a good product, can you reach the customer?
```
Judgment: (Reachable / Uncertain / Hard to reach)
Basis: (write here)
```

If there is a critical risk, what can you adjust to lower it?
