# Hypothesis & Research Table — For Aspiring Content Entrepreneurs (Customers, Competitors, Response)

> **Audience:** A worksheet for aspiring founders preparing a content business such as video, courses, e-books, newsletters, webtoons, or apps. If you already run a business and have your own sales and customer numbers, use the "Data Status Table" instead.

> **Nature:** An auxiliary worksheet for checking the business model hypotheses built in CH03 and CH05–CH08 against the research material you collected and simple response numbers. The metric names and formulas are proposals (〔Proposed〕) made for use in this worksheet, not standard definitions from the source knowledge notes.

> **Before you upload to an AI:** Write only public information and anonymous records you collected yourself in this table. Do not write the names, contact details, or account names of interview subjects.
