## 2. Value-Based Pricing (KM046)

- What problem does the customer solve, or what value do they feel they gain, from our product/service?
- What price range and willingness to pay will customers accept? (results of customer research/market response testing)
- How can we adjust cost and product composition so that the business is viable at that price?
- To justify a higher price, what basis of value (product, channel, service, brand) must we design and prove?

## 3. Competition-Based Pricing and Positioning (KM047)

- Who are the actual competitors (including substitutes) that offer similar customer value to the same customer segment as ours?
- What are those competitors' price levels?
- Are we choosing a low-price strategy or a high-price strategy? Are the conditions required by that choice in place (low price: accepting a lower operating margin and capital risk / high price: product, service, customer experience, channel, brand)?
- What is our answer to "what customer, and what value, do we want to be remembered for?" rather than "how much should we charge?"

## 4. Channel and Price Structure (KM048)

- Is the channel we use direct, indirect, or a mix?
- If we use an indirect channel, what are the fees and distribution margin, and are they reflected in the price structure?
- If we use a direct channel, do we have the resources to handle customer acquisition, shipping, and service ourselves?
- Do we need a price band between the consumer price and the actual sale price for discounts, commissions, or consignment sales fees? If so, how large should that band be?

## 5. Customer Relationship Cost (KM049)

- How much does it cost to acquire customers (personnel, systems, promotion)?
- How much does it cost to manage the purchase experience and drive repeat/additional purchases?
- Is this cost reflected in the current price structure (cost + margin)?

## 6. Revenue Streams and Revenue Model (KM050)

Answer the following questions.

- Who pays?
- What do they pay for?
- How much do they pay?
- When does the money come in?
- Do they pay once, or repeatedly?

## 7. One-Time Revenue vs. Recurring Revenue (KM051)

- Is our business a model where the relationship ends after selling the product once, or a model where continuous relationship management after purchase generates recurring revenue?
- If we carry fixed costs, must we acquire a new customer every time, or do existing customers generate revenue again?
- Beyond the initial purchase price, have we considered the cost required for the customer relationship together with the possibility of recurring revenue?
