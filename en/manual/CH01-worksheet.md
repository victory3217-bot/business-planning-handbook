# CH01 Business Development Criteria & Purpose Worksheet

> **How to use:** This worksheet can be completed on a client's own new-business project. Read "I / my" as your team, business unit, or in-house venture team rather than an individual founder.

> Source: Chapter 1 "Setting Your Business Criteria"
> This worksheet transfers the questions and judgment criteria on Business Development criteria and purpose directly into a fillable format. No new methodology has been added.
