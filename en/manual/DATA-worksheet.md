# Data Status Table — For Content Business Operators (Sales, Customers, Traffic)

> **Audience:** A worksheet for operators of content businesses such as video, courses, e-books, newsletters, webtoons, and apps. When a corporate new-business team reviews a content business, read "I / my" as "our team".

> **Nature:** An auxiliary worksheet for checking the business model hypotheses built in CH05–CH08 against your own actual numbers. The metric names and formulas are proposals (〔Proposed〕) made for use in this worksheet, not standard definitions from the source knowledge notes.

> **Before you upload to an AI:** Write only aggregated numbers in this table. Do not write customer names, contact details, email addresses, or account names.
