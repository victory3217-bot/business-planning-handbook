# CH01-business-criteria

- diagram_id: `business-criteria`
- chapter: CH01. 사업의 기준을 정한다 / Setting Your Business Criteria
- files: `ko/diagrams/CH01-business-criteria.svg`, `en/diagrams/CH01-business-criteria.svg`
- layout type: vertical flow, 480 x 712 units

## Learning purpose

Show that the entrepreneur's own criteria are brought into one priority system, that this system works as a filter and input for later decisions, and that the criteria are reopened when the outcome the entrepreneur wants changes.

## Source sections (CH01)

- Learning Objectives: "라이프스타일, 목표수익(매출/영업이익), 성장규모, Not-to-do라는 네 가지 개별 기준" / "four individual criteria: lifestyle, target revenue, growth scale, and Not-to-do".
- 2. 주요 프레임워크: "이 여러 기준 ... 은 하나의 우선순위 체계로 묶어볼 수 있다" ... "무엇을 우선할지는 사업가가 정한다".
- 6. 다음 Chapter와의 연결: "Not-to-do는 ... 아이템 선택 단계에서 직접 사용된다"; "사업목적과 목표수익은 ... 타당성 검토 단계의 전제조건으로 남는다"; "이 기준은 ... 거르는 필터로 계속 작동한다".
- 3. 적용 과정 and 5. 실무 적용 질문: 가족상황·자금상황·역할·시장환경이 달라지거나 원하는 결과 자체가 바뀌었을 때 "기준을 다시 열어야 할 때".

## Concepts included

사업의 기준 (사업가 자신의 판단기준); 라이프스타일; 목표수익; 성장규모; Not-to-do; 우선순위 체계; 아이템 선택; 시장·가격·타당성 검토; 재검토.

## Concepts intentionally excluded

- Risk tolerance, use of time, organization size, investor relations, social purpose: the chapter lists them as further criteria inside the priority system but defines only four as individual criteria. The diagram uses only the four.
- Any downstream stage beyond item selection and market/pricing/feasibility review.
- Any claim that execution data automatically updates the criteria. The chapter says execution data mainly revises detailed hypotheses and criteria are reopened only when the wanted outcome itself changes.
- No numbers, no internal source codes, no non-public source material.

## Relationship semantics

- Plain line from "사업의 기준" to the four chips: the criteria consist of these four items. No flow implied.
- Solid arrow from the four chips to "우선순위 체계": the individual criteria are brought together into one system.
- Solid arrow to the two downstream boxes, labeled "필터·입력값으로 작동": the system works as filter / input. "아이템 선택" is tied to Not-to-do being used directly; "시장·가격·타당성 검토" is tied to business purpose and target revenue as preconditions.
- Dashed gold arrow back to "사업의 기준": criteria are reopened when circumstances or the wanted outcome change.

EN: Business criteria consist of four criteria — lifestyle, target revenue, growth scale, and Not-to-do — brought together into a single priority system. This system works as a filter and an input in item selection and in the market, pricing, and feasibility review. The dashed arrow means the criteria are reopened when family circumstances, financial circumstances, role, or market environment change and the outcome the entrepreneur wants has changed.
