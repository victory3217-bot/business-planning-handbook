# CH08-execution-cycle

- diagram_id: `execution-cycle`

- files: `ko/diagrams/CH08-execution-cycle.svg`, `en/diagrams/CH08-execution-cycle.svg`
- layout type: vertical flow of five boxes with a dashed return loop, 480 x 800 units

## Learning purpose

Show the five questions that execution repeats as a cycle.

## Source sections

- 3. 적용 과정: 실행은 다섯 가지 질문을 반복하는 순환으로 운영할 수 있다. 검증 전, 실행 설계, 검증 후, 피보팅, 지식축적.
- 3: 이 질문에 답하고 관련 내용을 수정한 뒤 다시 실행하면 다음 사이클이 시작된다.
- 2. 검증설계의 4요소와 3. 피보팅 기준: 새로운 아이디어가 아니라 데이터가 기존 가설을 흔들었는가.

## Concepts included

The five questions; the three items of validation design named in step 2 (조사검증대상·측정지표·방법 및 계획); pivot basis; the next-cycle loop.

## Concepts intentionally excluded

AI as a supporting layer; Master Plan and back data details; business strategy to execution plan; numeric thresholds (the chapter gives none).

## Relationship semantics

Solid arrows: order of the five questions. Dashed gold arrow from the last to the first: after revising and executing again, the next cycle starts.

## KO labels

검증 전; 실행 설계; 검증 후; 피보팅; 지식축적.

## EN labels

Before Validation; Design the Execution; After Validation; Pivoting; Knowledge Accumulation.

## Color meaning

Teal = questions. Gold = Pivoting (decision). The dashed gold loop is the cycle restart.
