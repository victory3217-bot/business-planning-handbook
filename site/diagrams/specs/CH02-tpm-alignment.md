# CH02-tpm-alignment

- diagram_id: `tpm-alignment`
- chapter: CH02. 사업기회를 찾고 선택한다 / Finding and Selecting a Business Opportunity
- files: `ko/diagrams/CH02-tpm-alignment.svg`, `en/diagrams/CH02-tpm-alignment.svg`
- layout type: three-node triangle with a central alignment node, 480 x 740 units

A triangle was chosen over a Venn diagram: the chapter describes T, P, and M as elements that constrain and revise each other and must lock into one logic. It does not describe overlapping regions among the three.

## Learning purpose

Show that a business item is a provisional combination of T, P, and M, that the three constrain and revise one another, and that what matters is their alignment, not each element's individual score.

## Source sections (CH02)

- 1. 핵심 개념: "TPM은 사업계획의 골격을 T(무엇을 할 수 있는가), P(무엇을 팔 것인가), M(어디에서 사업기회를 찾을 것인가) 세 범주로 단순화한다"; "세 요소는 독립된 목록이 아니라 서로를 제약하고 수정하는 관계다."
- 2. 주요 프레임워크: "T: 실행 가능한 역량", "P: 상품가설", "M: 산업과 시장의 구분".
- 3. 적용 과정 4단계: "TPM의 핵심은 세 요소의 개별 점수가 아니라 정합성이다. 정합성이 확인되었다고 해서 사업아이템이 검증되었다는 뜻은 아니다."
- 3. 적용 과정 2단계: "최종적으로는 T·P·M이 하나의 논리로 맞물려야 한다."

## Concepts included

T 실행 가능한 역량 (무엇을 할 수 있는가); P 상품가설 (무엇을 팔 것인가); M 산업과 시장 (어디에서 사업기회를 찾을 것인가); 정합성; 정합성 확인은 사업 검증이 아니라는 점.

## Concepts intentionally excluded

- Top-down / Bottom-up and the T→P→M / M→P→T paths (would complicate the core diagram).
- Three risks (market, product, channel), PEST, 5-Forces, value chain, industry map.
- "T needs distinctiveness, P needs value, M needs attractiveness": not drawn, to keep three equal nodes.
- Any mapping of M to "external opportunity" as a strict label, since the chapter uses that phrase for the intersection logic, not as the definition of M.

## Relationship semantics

- Two-way arrows between each pair (T-M, T-P, M-P): the elements constrain and revise each other. No sequence implied.
- Gold node "정합성" in the center: T, P, and M lock into one logic. It is a state, not a fourth element, so it carries no arrows.
- Teal note: alignment confirmation is not business validation; it signals the hypothesis is clear enough for the next stage.

## Color meaning

Navy = the three core elements. Gold = alignment (the key relationship). Teal = supporting note.

## Accessibility description (SVG `<desc>`)

KO: T(실행 가능한 역량, 무엇을 할 수 있는가), P(상품가설, 무엇을 팔 것인가), M(산업과 시장, 어디에서 사업기회를 찾을 것인가) 세 요소가 서로를 제약하고 수정하는 관계로 연결되어 있고, 세 요소가 하나의 논리로 맞물릴 때 정합성이 만들어진다. 정합성 확인은 사업 검증 완료가 아니라 다음 단계로 넘어갈 만큼 가설이 선명해졌다는 신호다.

EN: T (executable capability: what can you do), P (product hypothesis: what will you sell), and M (industry and market: where will you look for the business opportunity) are connected as elements that constrain and revise each other. Alignment is created when the three lock together into one logic. Confirming alignment is not business validation; it is a signal that the hypothesis is clear enough to move to the next stage.
