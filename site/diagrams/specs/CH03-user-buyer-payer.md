# CH03-user-buyer-payer

- diagram_id: `user-buyer-payer`
- chapter: CH03. 고객·구매자·문제를 정의한다 / Defining the Customer, the Buyer, and the Problem
- files: `ko/diagrams/CH03-user-buyer-payer.svg`, `en/diagrams/CH03-user-buyer-payer.svg`
- layout type: three-column role definitions above two stacked bands (converged / split), 480 x 430 units

## Learning purpose

Distinguish who experiences the problem, who decides the purchase, and who bears the cost, and show that these roles can sit with one person or be split across different people.

## Source sections (CH03)

- 1. 핵심 개념: "실제로 문제를 겪는 사용자(User), 구매를 결정하는 구매자(Buyer), 비용을 지불하는 지불자(Payer)는 B2C에서는 한 사람에게 모일 수 있지만, B2B·B2G에서는 현장 사용자, 구매담당자, 예산승인자로 분리되는 경우가 많다."
- 3. 적용 과정 2단계: "사용자·구매자·지불자가 같은 사람인지, 다른 사람인지 확인한다."
- 핵심 정리: "사용자·구매자·지불자는 같을 수도 다를 수도 있으며, 최소한 이 세 역할을 구분해야 한다."

## Concepts included

User / Buyer / Payer and their one-line meanings; B2C (can converge into one person); B2B·B2G (frequently split into field user, purchasing manager, budget approver).

## Concepts intentionally excluded

- Problem existence and severity, attributes, KBF, touchpoints, segmentation.
- Buying logic per transaction type (individual need / organizational performance / budget, statute, procurement).
- The advice to translate effects into the Buyer's language of cost, performance, and risk.
- Any claim about B2B or B2G beyond the chapter's own sentence ("많다" / "frequently" is kept, not "always").

## Relationship semantics

- No arrows. The three role cards at the top define the roles.
- Gold-outlined band: in B2C the three roles can converge into one person ("can", not "always").
- Teal cards: in B2B·B2G the roles are frequently split; each card shows the role it fills (role name small, example label bold).
