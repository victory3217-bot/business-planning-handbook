# CH05-business-model-structure

- diagram_id: `business-model-structure`
- chapter: CH05. 비즈니스모델을 설계한다 / Designing the Business Model
- files: `ko/diagrams/CH05-business-model-structure.svg`, `en/diagrams/CH05-business-model-structure.svg`
- layout type: three nodes around a central axis node, 480 x 740 units

## Learning purpose

Show that the Value Proposition is the axis of the business model and that value delivery, value creation, and value capture cannot be designed independently.

## Source sections

- 2.1: 가치제안을 축으로 비즈니스모델은 가치전달, 가치창출, 가치회수라는 세 구조로 나뉘며, 이 셋은 서로 분리된 채 독립적으로 설계될 수 없다.
- 2.1 members: 가치전달 (고객·채널·고객관계), 가치창출 (핵심자원·핵심활동·핵심파트너십), 가치회수 (비용구조와 수익원).
- 2.2: 연결문장이 자연스럽게 이어지지 않으면 캔버스 어딘가의 의사결정이 아직 정리되지 않았다는 뜻이다.

## Concepts included

가치제안 (axis); 가치전달; 가치창출; 가치회수 and their listed members; the interaction note.

## Concepts intentionally excluded

The nine-box BMC grid; T-canvas; Business Model Story; cost calculation and pricing (deferred to CH06); How to Sell / How to Make; pairwise arrows between the three structures (the chapter states interaction only in general terms).

## Relationship semantics

Plain lines from the Value Proposition to each structure: connected around the axis, no direction implied. Teal note: a choice in one box changes the others; a broken connecting sentence signals an unresolved decision.
