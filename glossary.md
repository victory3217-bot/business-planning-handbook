## Notes for Future Review

- 결정력 (one of the seven competitive-advantage criteria, CH04) was translated as "Decisiveness" — the Korean term sits between "decision-influencing power" and "decisiveness"; the closer single-word noun form was chosen for parallelism with the other six criteria.
- 소화제 (CH04 example) was translated literally as "digestive medicine" — no single natural English equivalent exists ("digestive aid" / "antacid" are close alternatives).
- 『스타트업을 하는 깐깐한 방법』 (book title cited in CH04) was translated as *The Meticulous Way to Do a Startup* as a working title only; no official English title exists. This is a translation of the title string itself, not new content.
